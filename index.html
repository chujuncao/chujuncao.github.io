EVENT & SOCIAL APP

EventJoy — local events for newcomers

A mobile app that helps newly-landed international students find local events they can actually trust — clear organizer info, honest event details, and a sense of who else is going, so attending alone feels less intimidating.

MY ROLE

Full team project across research, prototyping, and testing. I authored the background research individually, ran part of the interviews and usability testing, and built the final high-fidelity mockup on my own.

COOKING & SOCIAL APP

FlyingSaucers — cooking rituals across the distance

An app for people who used to cook with someone they love and now live too far apart to do it in person. Friends or family follow the same recipe on their own schedule, then share photos and reactions — no video call required.

MY ROLE

Group project (research & concept) → individual continuation. I led usability testing, synthesized findings into design changes, and finalized the visual direction.

INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

The Well-Faceted Coffee Shop

A coffee retailer's storefront came with every possible filter — cultivar, altitude, acidity, two brewing fields. Technically complete, practically unusable. I cut it down to what people actually use, then designed two different ways to browse the same catalog.

MY ROLE

Individual project. Competitive research, both IA structures, and a discount usability test are all my own work.

ABOUT

Research first,
then pixels.

profile.jpg

Curiosity got me into UX. Empathy keeps me here.

A class project once asked me to design a platform connecting people without a car to on-demand couriers. We only got as far as research — but I couldn't stop imagining what it would feel like to actually use it.

My HR background sharpened that empathy. Media Studies showed me how the same environment can mean very different things to different people. Both are why I design research-first, for people mainstream apps often overlook — and why I keep returning to tools for connection, at a time when meeting in person has gotten harder.
